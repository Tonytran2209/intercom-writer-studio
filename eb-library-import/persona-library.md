# Persona Library

Used in Step 2 to decide which persona(s) a piece of EB content is likely to resonate with, based on stated motivation and mindset — not job title matching alone. Sourced from Chi's own persona documentation.

## 1. L&D Executive
- **Core motivation:** turn confusing knowledge into clear content; create real impact through learning/communication; build better learning and onboarding systems; become a voice on learning clarity within the team.
- **Mindset:** always asks "what do people actually need to understand?"; likes breaking down concepts, sequences, logic; cares how visual/media serves learning; dislikes long-but-vague content; likes finding best practice and adapting it to context.
- **Working style:** collaborates across teams to clarify problems; structured — likes outlines, frameworks, mapping; writes and researches well; refines through feedback; proactively improves internal systems rather than just completing assigned tasks.
- **Growth expectation:** learning design lead / content strategy lead; builder of learning systems and internal capability; clarity/structuring consultant.
- **Resonates with content about:** systems thinking, clarity-driven problem solving, turning mess into structure, internal capability building.

## 2. Account Executive
- **Core motivation:** close deals through consultative thinking, not just follow-up; understand clients deeply enough to propose the right solution; build trust and keep momentum to signature; grow into someone who both sells and sees the business problem.
- **Mindset:** treats a brief as a problem to solve, not a single request; curious about pain points, buying triggers, decision paths; energized by moving a deal from "maybe" to "yes"; treats rejection as data; cares about client outcomes, not just sending quotes.
- **Working style:** clear, confident, direct communication; strategic persistent follow-up; coordinates with production/L&D to promise accurately; keeps CRM/pipeline/proposal flow organized; learns from each deal to improve the next.
- **Growth expectation:** senior consultative seller; key account / account strategist; leader of large deals and long-term client growth.
- **Resonates with content about:** deal-closing energy, client trust-building, growth through iteration.

## 3. Business Development
- **Core motivation:** build a repeatable BD engine, not one dependent on luck; find which segment/message/offer actually converts; understand why deals convert or fail, not just close them; drive revenue growth through test → learn → improve.
- **Mindset:** thinks in hypotheses, experiments, signals, conversion; prefers digging into buyer insight over mass outreach; treats rejection/open-rate/response-rate/deal-loss as data; optimizes systems over acting on gut feel; cares about deal quality and velocity, not vanity metrics.
- **Working style:** researches segments, builds angles, tests offers; hunts leads and personally validates via discovery; runs multiple campaigns in parallel with clear logic; documents learning and refines messaging; uses AI/tools to speed up thinking, writing, testing.
- **Growth expectation:** growth-oriented BD lead; revenue/pipeline builder; builder of outbound and conversion systems for the company.
- **Resonates with content about:** experimentation culture, data-driven iteration, building systems that compound.

## 4. AI Engineer — Automation & Business Solutions Specialist
- **Core motivation:** turn messy manual work into systems that run and are measurable; create real capacity (not just launch tools for their own sake) — reduce dependency on people, reduce rework; be trusted to decide a solution from an ambiguous problem brief without hand-holding; become someone a team lead can hand a vague problem to directly.
- **Mindset:** always asks "where's the real bottleneck — process, knowledge, tooling, or none at all?"; diagnoses before building, dislikes building without understanding the problem; matches solution size to problem size — no over-engineering, no band-aids; dislikes "connecting tools for the sake of it" — wants tools that produce measurable outcomes; tests against real cases and evidence rather than assumption.
- **Working style:** sits directly with team leads/users to extract real rules and examples, not just written requirements; structures work as input/logic/output/where human judgment stays; builds the first version themself, supports rollout, observes where users get stuck; pushes back on requirements that don't serve the outcome.
- **Growth expectation:** automation/solutions lead — designer of internal systems, not just an operator of tools; a voice in deciding what's worth automating; a trusted technical-business bridge in the organization.
- **Resonates with content about:** diagnosing before building, ownership of ambiguous problems, systems thinking, measurable impact.

## 5. Social Series Content Strategist
- **Core motivation:** turn dry expertise into content people actually stop and watch; create real impact through content — not just "enough posts," but a series that's remembered, followed, shared; build a repeatable content system (a series), not one-off scheduled tasks; lead the full lifecycle of a series — from research to results, not just the writing.
- **Mindset:** always asks "why is this content remembered, and that one scrolled past?"; looks for repeatable hooks, formats, patterns — not just copying whatever trend is hot; cares how storytelling serves understanding, not just entertainment; dislikes long-but-shallow content — wants clear structure: concept, audience, hook, format, pipeline; studies platform best practices (TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube) and adapts them to the right context and audience.
- **Working style:** proactively researches audience pain points, competitors, trends, effective formats before writing; works systematically — defines series concept, audience, hook, topic pipeline before production; writes clear briefs for designers/animators/editors and leads the production team, not just the writing; reviews performance, extracts insight (which hooks worked, which series to expand), then improves; balances creative instinct with structure.
- **Growth expectation:** content strategy lead / social series lead; builder of an internal content system that makes expertise easy to understand and spread; a voice in deciding "what content is worth making, and why."
- **Resonates with content about:** storytelling craft, content systems, hook/format experimentation, creative + structure balance.

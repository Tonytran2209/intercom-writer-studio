# EVP Pillar Library

**Status: confirmed by Chi (Sep 2026).** These are the 3 pillars to map against. If a piece's fit is still genuinely ambiguous between two pillars, ask Chi which one it argues for most — the definitions are settled, the per-piece call may not be.

## Pillar 1 — Analytical & Clarity-driven
**Definition:** values structured thinking, problem-solving rigor, clarity over cleverness — turning something messy or complex into something well-organized and understandable.
**Likely fits:** content about systems, frameworks, how the team thinks through a problem, diagnosis-before-action stories. Connects most directly to the L&D Executive and AI Engineer personas' stated mindsets.

## Pillar 2 — Energetic & Spirited
**Definition:** values fun, team energy, informal culture, creative spark, human connection outside of pure output.
**Likely fits:** content about team moments, shared spaces, events, humor, spontaneity. The chill-spaces reference article maps here (see `article-library.md` for the full confirmed set).

## Pillar 3 — Ownership & Growth
**Definition:** values autonomy, initiative, career development, being trusted with ambiguity rather than told exactly what to do.
**Likely fits:** content about career paths, taking on responsibility, building something and being trusted with it. Connects to the growth-expectation language across all 5 personas (wanting to become leads, build systems, be trusted with ambiguous problems).

---

**Note for Step 2:** a single piece can gesture at more than one pillar, but should be tagged with the one it argues for *most directly* — the same discipline the outline-generator applies to its own principle selection (if all three apply equally, the piece is too broad).
